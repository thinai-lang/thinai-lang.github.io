description: Explore the Thinai language before its first alpha release.

Thinai is a domain-specific modeling language for domain-driven design. The guides on this site introduce the language and its current syntax.

Thinai is under active development and does not yet have a published alpha release. Installation and editor setup instructions will be added with the first release. Until then, use the [language guides](index.md) to explore the model and follow the [project repository](https://github.com/thinai-lang/thinai) for development updates.
